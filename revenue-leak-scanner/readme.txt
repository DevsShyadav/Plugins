== Description ==

**Your store is leaking money. Revenue Leak Scanner shows you exactly where.**

Most WooCommerce store owners are losing $500-$5,000+ per month from preventable issues — and they don't even know it. Abandoned carts? Slow checkout? Missing trust signals? Broken mobile experience? You're blind to these problems without the right tool.

Revenue Leak Scanner performs a comprehensive one-click audit of your entire store and does something no other tool does: **puts a dollar amount next to every problem.**

= What Makes This Different =

Other tools tell you "your site is slow." We tell you **"you're losing $847/month because your checkout page takes 4.2 seconds to load."**

= What Gets Scanned =

* **Checkout Flow** — Guest checkout, form fields, payment methods, shipping options, coupon field optimization
* **Product Pages** — Missing images, reviews, descriptions, upsells/cross-sells, out-of-stock visibility
* **Performance** — Page speed, image optimization, caching, database bloat, plugin count
* **Mobile Experience** — Viewport configuration, touch targets, mobile checkout, search functionality
* **SEO** — Meta descriptions, permalinks, alt tags, SSL certificate
* **Trust Signals** — Privacy policy, refund policy, contact info, trust badges, testimonials

== Installation ==

1. Upload the `revenue-leak-scanner` folder to `/wp-content/plugins/`
2. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' menu
3. Navigate to 'Revenue Leaks' in your admin sidebar
4. Click 'Run Your First Scan' and wait ~30 seconds
5. Review your results and start fixing leaks!

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Does this slow down my site? =

No. Scans run only when you trigger them (or on schedule). They do not affect frontend performance.

= How accurate are the dollar amounts? =

Revenue estimates are calculated using your actual store data (orders, revenue, traffic) combined with industry-standard conversion benchmarks from Baymard Institute, Google, and Nielsen research. The more data your store has, the more accurate the estimates.

= Does it work with WooCommerce HPOS? =

Yes! The plugin is fully compatible with High-Performance Order Storage.

= Can I schedule automatic scans? =

Yes. Go to Settings and choose daily, weekly, or monthly automatic scans with optional email reports.
